IRIS sorting study

IRIS is a robot arm that sorts used surgical instruments from a tray into their positions in the set tray, so staff only need to check the result. We would like your opinion on how it works and on its screen.

  • Takes under 10 minutes: a short video, four quick tasks on a clickable screen, and a few questions.
  • Anonymous. We ask for your position, not your name. We record your answers and how long the survey took, nothing else.
  • The clickable screen is not connected to a robot. Nothing you press moves anything.
  • We built IRIS, so problems you find are the most useful answers.
  • Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time. Questions: .

The screen works best on a computer or tablet. On a phone, turn it sideways.
