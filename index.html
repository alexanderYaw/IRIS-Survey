IRIS sorting study

IRIS is a prototype robotic system designed to sort surgical instruments from an input tray into designated positions in a receiving tray. This study asks for your feedback on the proposed workflow and operator screen.

  • Estimated time: about 10 minutes. You will watch a short video, try four short tasks on a clickable screen, and answer a few questions.
  • We do not ask for your name or email address. We collect your responses, a generated response ID, the survey start time, and total time taken. Your answers are temporarily saved in this browser so you can continue if the page reloads; they are removed from the browser when you send them.
  • The clickable screen is not connected to a robot. Your actions affect only the on-screen simulation.
  • We are evaluating the design. Please tell us what works, what does not, and what is missing.
  • Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time. Questions: .

Four short tasks

Next you will use a proposed new design of the operator's screen. Each task opens it at a different moment. Do what you would do at work, then press Return to questions, whether or not you finished the task.

We are testing the design, not your performance. If you are unsure what to do or cannot complete a task, that is useful feedback.

The screen works best on a computer or tablet. On a phone, turn it sideways.
